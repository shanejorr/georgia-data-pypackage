# Articles

Longer worked examples, each run against the live API when the site is
built and saved with its output.

- [Analyzing Georgia data](https://georgiacivicdata.org/py/articles/analysis.md): put infant mortality and the
  poverty rate side by side, check that two datasets share a key and years,
  read a dataset's notes, and pool rates from their counts.
- [School zones and places](https://georgiacivicdata.org/py/articles/school-zones.md): map a district's zones,
  find schools without a zone, check how precise the zones are, and locate
  points and addresses.

The [Get started](https://georgiacivicdata.org/py/articles/get-started.md) article covers the basics: the
catalog, names and filters, and the rules of the data.
