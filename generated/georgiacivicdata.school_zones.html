georgiacivicdata.school_zones#

georgiacivicdata.school_zones(district_code=None, school_code=None, district=None, school=None, zone_level=None, grade=None, year=None, resolution='display', *, geometry=True, backend=None)#

Get school attendance zones.

Attendance-zone polygons: the areas that assign a home to its zoned elementary, middle and high school, one polygon per school, level and grade run, for the current school year.

Zones differ in precision. Some are the district's official GIS layers; others are digitized from PDF maps, rebuilt from street lists, or are the whole district outline (districts with one school per grade). Read method and accuracy_class before treating zones as equally exact, and see school_zone_coverage() for how completely each district was collected. A zone is the default assignment, not an official enrollment determination: confirm with the district.

A statewide pull is large, especially at resolution="full". Filter to a district and keep the "display" default for maps.

Parameters:

  • district_code (str or list of str, optional) -- Codes.

  • school_code (str or list of str, optional) -- Codes.

  • district (str or list of str, optional) -- Names, matched to codes as in rows(). A school name found in more than one district needs district too.

  • school (str or list of str, optional) -- Names, matched to codes as in rows(). A school name found in more than one district needs district too.

  • zone_level (str or list of str, optional) -- "elementary", "middle" and/or "high". Default: all.

  • grade (str or int, optional) -- One grade: keep zones serving it ("pk", "k", "01" to "12"; "5", 5 and "K" are accepted). Default: all.

  • year (int, optional) -- The zones' school year as its ending year (2027 is 2026-27). Default: the current zones.

  • resolution ({"display", "full"}, default "display") -- "display": simplified polygons for maps, much smaller; "full": the source polygons the point lookup uses.

  • geometry (bool, default True) -- True returns a geopandas GeoDataFrame (needs the geo extra: pip install georgiacivicdata[geo]); False returns the attributes as a plain data frame.

  • backend ({"pandas", "polars"}, optional) -- With geometry=False, the kind of data frame to return (default: the backend option). Geometry is always a GeoDataFrame.

Returns:

geopandas.GeoDataFrame or DataFrame -- One row per zone, with columns year (integer), district_code, district_name, school_code, school_name, zone_level, grade_low, grade_high ("pk", "k", "01" ... "12"), method (how the zone was built), accuracy_class, currency_status, boundary_vintage, source_url, retrieved_on (date), area_sq_mi, the bounding box (bbox_xmin, bbox_ymin, bbox_xmax, bbox_ymax), source_school_name (the name as the district's map spells it), then positional_rmse_m, currency_note, source_publisher, nces_school_id, district_census_id, nces_lea_id and the geometry (WGS 84). The API's disclaimer is meta(x)["note"].

Examples

>>> import georgiacivicdata as gcd
>>> zones = gcd.school_zones(
...     district="Atlanta Public Schools", zone_level="high"
... )
>>> zones.plot(column="school_name")
>>> gcd.school_zones(district_code="630", geometry=False)
